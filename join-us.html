How to get started

  1. Submit Your handle Provide your Codeforces username. This helps us gauge your activity and group you with comparable practice partners.
  2. Establish a streak Competitive programming requires daily rigor. Solve at least 1-2 problems daily to maintain analytical agility.
  3. Attend mock rounds Join our weekly simulated rounds to adapt to the speed and high pressure constraints of actual contest environments.
  4. Collaborate & Learn Participate in post-contest discussions. Reviewing editorial solutions from senior members is the fastest way to progress.
